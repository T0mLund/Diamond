The view from BEING

The eye stays at BEING. Drag to pan and tilt the cone around the spheres. Pinch or scroll to narrow the cone as it moves outward through the names, then widen it to return toward the centre.

Names keep a fixed size in the model, so narrowing magnifies them. Closer layers dissolve as the cone passes them; farther layers remain faintly present.

Three concentric ripples mark each axis–sphere crossing. They are larger on larger spheres.

Vocabulary. The three spheres are Responsiveness, Involvement and Fixation: how tightly a position is held, not how much of anything there is. The three axes are Rejecting and Grasping (across), Self-diminishment and Self-elevation (up and down in the equator), and Insistence on continuance and on disappearance (the long axis, with Permanence and Erasure at its tips). Birth, Death and BEING are unchanged, and Birth is not Permanence, nor Death Erasure.

A contemplative mandala in a modern visual language: a proposal, not a traditional Buddhist system and not a measurement. Landmark coordinates are unchanged.
