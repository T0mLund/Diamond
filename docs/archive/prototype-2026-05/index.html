Graves' Diamond

An experimental, AI-assisted model.

A model of the experienced universe. Everything emanates from and returns to BEING.

Awakening and Obliteration exist together on the y axis; Past and Future is the x axis; Love and Hate is the z axis.

Birth and Death emerge from BEING to structure the experienced universe. They then sit just past its bounds, where only Awakening and Obliteration can reach.

Drag to orbit, scroll inward. At closer zoom levels there are clickable references, approximately mapped to the interior coordinates (AI-generated).
